## List of Experiments

### ✔ Experiment 1 – Deep Learning Fundamentals & Optimization  
Study of neural network structure, loss functions, and optimization algorithms like Gradient Descent.

---

### ✔ Experiment 2 – Overfitting and Regularization  
Analysis of model overfitting and implementation of techniques such as Dropout and L1/L2 regularization.

---

### ✔ Experiment 3 – Learning Rate Strategies & Early Stopping  
Understanding the role of learning rate and applying early stopping to improve model performance.

---

### ✔ Experiment 4 – Convolutional Neural Networks  
Implementation of CNN architecture for image classification and feature extraction.

---

### ✔ Experiment 5 – Transfer Learning  
Use of pre-trained models to enhance performance and reduce training time.

---

### ✔ Experiment 6 – Sequential Models (RNN, LSTM, GRU)  
Working with sequential data and comparing different architectures for sequence learning.

---

### ✔ Experiment 8 – Model Explainability using LIME  
Interpretation of model predictions using LIME to understand feature contributions.

### ✔ Experiment 9 – Variational Autoencoders (VAE) vs Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
Comparative study of generative models including VAE and GAN architectures for synthetic image generation.

**Topics Covered:**
- Encoder–Decoder architecture  
- Latent space representation  
- GAN Generator and Discriminator  
- Image generation using deep generative models  
- Comparison of VAE and GAN outputs
